# Riverbend Community Clinic: Hypertension Follow-up Policy
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. Fictional clinic policy for software testing. Not clinical guidance.

A patient with a blood pressure reading of 140/90 or higher at two visits gets a recheck visit within 14 days.
The patient keeps a home blood pressure log for 7 days before the recheck. A nurse calls on day 10 to confirm the log is being kept.
A reading of 180/120 or higher with symptoms such as chest pain or severe headache needs a same-day urgent visit.
Medications for hypertension are reviewed every 6 months.
