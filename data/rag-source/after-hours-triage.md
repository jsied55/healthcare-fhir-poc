# Riverbend Community Clinic: After-Hours Triage Policy
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. Fictional clinic policy for software testing. Not clinical guidance.

The after-hours nurse line runs from 6 PM to 7 AM. The on-call clinician calls back within 30 minutes.
Callers with chest pain or trouble breathing are told to call 911 first.
The on-call clinician documents every after-hours call in the record within 24 hours.
