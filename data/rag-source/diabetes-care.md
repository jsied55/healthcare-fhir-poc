# Riverbend Community Clinic: Diabetes Care Policy
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. Fictional clinic policy for software testing. Not clinical guidance.

A foot exam with a monofilament test is done every 12 months. Patients rated high risk for foot problems get the exam every 3 months.
A1C is checked every 3 months until the patient reaches the clinic target of below 7.5 percent, then every 6 months.
Dilated eye exams are referred out once a year. Patients get a written care plan at their annual visit.
