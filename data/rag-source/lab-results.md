# Riverbend Community Clinic: Lab Result Notification Policy
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. Fictional clinic policy for software testing. Not clinical guidance.

Normal results are posted to the patient portal within 5 business days. Abnormal results get a phone call within 2 business days.
Critical values are reported to the patient within 1 hour of the clinician seeing them.
Results are released to the portal only after 72 hours, so the clinician can review them first.
