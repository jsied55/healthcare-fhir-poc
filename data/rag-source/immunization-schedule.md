# Riverbend Community Clinic: Adult Immunization Policy
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. Fictional clinic policy for software testing. Not clinical guidance.

The flu shot is offered every year from September 1 through November 30.
A tetanus booster is given every 10 years. The shingles vaccine is two doses, 2 to 6 months apart, for patients age 50 and over.
Nurse-only vaccine visits need no appointment on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 8 AM to 11 AM.
